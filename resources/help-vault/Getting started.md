# Getting started

## Notes and folders

- **Ctrl+N** makes a new note in the current folder. Type its name and press Enter.
- Right-click in the file tree for new folders and canvases, renaming, moving and deleting.
- Drag files and folders in the tree to move them. Links to them are updated for you.
- Ctrl+click or Shift+click selects several files at once.

## Finding things

- **Ctrl+O** (quick switcher) opens a note by typing part of its name, path or alias. Shift+Enter creates a note with that name.
- **Ctrl+Shift+F** searches every note. Use `"exact phrases"`, `-exclude`, `/regex/`, `path:`, `file:` and `tag:#name`.
- The left sidebar has **Bookmarks** (files and searches) and **Tags**; the right sidebar has **Backlinks**, **Outgoing links**, the **Outline** and a **Local graph** of the open note.

## Tabs and panes

- Notes open in tabs. Ctrl+Tab moves between them, Ctrl+W closes one.
- Right-click a tab to split right or down, or drag a tab to the edge of a pane.
- **Alt+←** and **Alt+→** go back and forward through the notes a pane showed.
- Pin a tab from its menu to keep it open.

## Views

- **Live Preview** (the default) shows formatting as you type and reveals the Markdown where your cursor is.
- **Ctrl+E** switches to **Reading view**; the command "Toggle Source mode" shows plain Markdown.
