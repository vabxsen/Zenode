# Welcome to Zenode

Zenode keeps your notes as plain Markdown files in a folder on your own disk. That folder is called a **vault**. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and any other editor can open the same files.

This Help vault is a working example: open the notes, follow the links, and try things out. It is replaced with a fresh copy when Zenode updates, so keep your own notes in your own vault.
